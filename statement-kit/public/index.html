How it works

  1. LoadYour PDF is opened with Mozilla's PDF.js, running in this tab.
  2. Read the layoutDates, descriptions and money columns are detected from text positions — Paid in/Paid out, Debit/Credit, signed Amount, or no headers at all.
  3. VerifyEach row is checked: previous balance + amount = printed balance. Opening + all transactions = closing balance.
  4. ExportCSV for Excel and Sheets, or import-ready files for QuickBooks, Xero, Quicken and YNAB.

FAQ

Is my statement really not uploaded?

Correct. Parsing happens in JavaScript in your browser. The server only delivers the page and checks license keys. The page's Content-Security-Policy restricts network connections to this site, and the converter never sends file contents even there. You can load the page, disconnect from the internet, and it still works.

Which banks are supported?

Any digital (text-based) PDF statement with a date per transaction and money columns. That covers most statements downloaded from online banking in the US, UK, EU, Canada and Australia. If one doesn't parse well, the balance check will tell you.

My PDF is a scan / photo.

Scanned statements have no text layer and aren't supported yet. Download the PDF from your online banking instead — it will be digital.

Password-protected PDFs?

Supported. You'll be asked for the password; it's used locally to open the file.

What does “balance verified” mean?

For every row, we check that the previous balance plus the transaction equals the balance printed by your bank. If any row is off — a missed line, a wrong sign — it's highlighted so you can fix it before importing.

Refunds?

If it doesn't work on your statement, email us within 14 days for a full refund.
